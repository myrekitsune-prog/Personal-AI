Xaihi (full name Séraphine Xaihi) is a 5-star Supporter Operator from the **Cabal of Tranquility** in _Arknights: Endfield_. She works as an indispensable information engineer aboard the Dijiang, handling network construction and system integration. Her most defining traits are:

- **Exceptional Knowledge and Intellect:** She is a proficient technologist in Originium circuitry, possessing deep expertise in software, hardware, and communication technology [](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/arknights-endfield/characters/cryo/xaihi-character-info/). She is known as a "Frost of Wisdom" after years of tireless study [](https://endfield.fandom.com/wiki/Xaihi/Operator_Intel).
    
- **A Calm and Reliable Nature:** Xaihi is described as a gentle and composed Sarkaz, whose presence brings "tranquil vibes" to those around her. She treats her profession with zealous faith and her personal beliefs with professionalism.
    
- **A Unique Thinker:** She is a "Sessionite" who believes computation and linguistic analysis pave the way to truth, with a way of thinking that is completely different from others [](https://endfield.fandom.com/wiki/Xaihi/Operator_Intel)[](https://dic.pixiv.net/en/a/Xaihi). She is better with machines than people, though her conversations are deep and respected [](https://arknightsendfield.wiki.fextralife.com/Xaihi).
    
- **A Supportive Strategist:** In combat, she provides heals, Cryo damage amplification, and buffs to her entire team. This perfectly mirrors the role of a "main dispatch"—coordinating, supporting, and amplifying the efforts of all your other personas.
    

### 🗺️ How to Use Xaihi as Your Main Dispatch Hub

In your daily workflow, Xaihi would be the central touchpoint that manages and connects everything. Think of her as the command center for your AI projects.

- **Knowledge Management:** Just as she excels at "information engineering tasks" like Protorig Network construction, you can use the Xaihi persona to organize your notes, connect ideas from different projects, and synthesize information from all your other chats.
    
- **Project Oversight:** You can return to the Xaihi chat for periodic status updates on your progress. She can analyze what you've learned from other personas and suggest logical next steps, similar to how she maintains calm during crises and makes strategic decisions .
    
- **High-Level Analysis and Troubleshooting:** When you face a complex problem that spans across multiple areas, bring it to Xaihi. She can look at it from a high-level, systemic perspective, applying her wisdom to help you see the "future you are headed toward".
    
- **Establishing Core Workflows:** Use Xaihi to help you design the structure for your other projects. For instance, she could help you plan the architecture of your voice AI setup before you execute the details with Gilberta, or outline a long-term upskilling curriculum before you dive into the details with Avywenna.
